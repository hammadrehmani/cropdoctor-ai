# Cotton Disease Knowledge Base

## Cotton Leaf Curl Disease (CLCuD)

Cotton Leaf Curl Disease is the most devastating disease of cotton in Pakistan. It is caused by a begomovirus transmitted by the silverleaf whitefly (Bemisia tabaci).

### Symptoms
- Upward or downward curling of leaves
- Dark, thickened veins on the undersides of leaves
- Enations (leaf-like outgrowths) on the underside of leaves
- Stunted plant growth
- Reduced boll formation

### Favourable Conditions
- Warm temperatures (25–35°C)
- High whitefly populations
- Dry conditions with low humidity

### Management
1. Use CLCuD-resistant varieties (NIAB-878, MNH-886, CIM-598)
2. Control whitefly with imidacloprid 70WS (seed treatment) or thiamethoxam
3. Remove infected plants early to reduce spread
4. Avoid late sowing — early planted crops escape peak whitefly pressure
5. Monitor fields twice weekly during vegetative stage

## Bacterial Blight of Cotton

Caused by Xanthomonas citri subsp. malvacearum.

### Symptoms
- Water-soaked spots on cotyledons and young leaves
- Angular, dark-brown lesions on leaves
- Black arm: dark, water-soaked lesions on stems and bolls
- Boll rot in severe cases

### Management
1. Use certified, disease-free seed
2. Seed treatment with streptomycin sulphate (200 ppm)
3. Spray copper oxychloride (3 g/L) at first symptom appearance
4. Crop rotation with non-host crops

## Alternaria Leaf Spot

Caused by Alternaria macrospora. Common under high humidity conditions.

### Symptoms
- Small, circular brown spots with concentric rings
- Yellow halo around lesions
- Premature leaf drop in severe cases

### Management
1. Apply mancozeb (2.5 g/L) or iprodione foliar sprays
2. Improve field drainage
3. Avoid overhead irrigation
