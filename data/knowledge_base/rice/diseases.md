# Rice Disease Knowledge Base

## Rice Blast

Caused by Magnaporthe oryzae. The most damaging rice disease worldwide.

### Symptoms
- Leaf blast: Diamond-shaped lesions with grey centres and brown margins on leaves
- Neck blast: Rotting at the base of the panicle (most damaging — can destroy entire yield)
- Node blast: Dark brown/black lesions on stem nodes

### Favourable Conditions
- Night temperatures 17–20°C, day temperatures 26–28°C
- High relative humidity (>90%)
- Excessive nitrogen fertilisation
- Cloudy weather / low solar radiation

### Management
1. Use blast-resistant varieties (Super Basmati, KS-282 where available)
2. Avoid excessive nitrogen — split application recommended
3. Apply tricyclazole 75WP (0.6 g/L) at:
   - Tillering stage (preventive)
   - Booting stage (critical — protects neck)
4. Drain fields periodically to reduce humidity

## Brown Spot

Caused by Bipolaris oryzae. Often associated with nutrient-stressed crops.

### Symptoms
- Oval to circular brown spots with yellow halos on leaves
- Spots may coalesce under severe infection
- Grain discolouration (stackburn) on infected panicles

### Favourable Conditions
- Temperatures 25–30°C
- Nutrient-deficient soils (especially potassium deficiency)
- Intermittent wet/dry periods

### Management
1. Apply balanced fertilisation (N:P:K) — correct potassium deficiency
2. Spray mancozeb or iprodione at early infection
3. Use healthy, certified seed

## Bacterial Leaf Blight (BLB)

Caused by Xanthomonas oryzae pv. oryzae.

### Symptoms
- Leaf margins turn yellow and straw-coloured starting at leaf tips
- Water-soaked lesions along leaf margins
- "Kresek" phase: young seedlings wilt and die

### Management
1. No effective chemical control — prevention is key
2. Use BLB-resistant varieties
3. Avoid flood irrigation after storm damage
4. Do not apply high doses of nitrogen
