# Rice Disease Management Guide (English)

## Rice Blast — Magnaporthe oryzae (Pyricularia oryzae)
- **Crop**: Rice
- **Symptoms**: Spindle- or diamond-shaped lesions with greyish-white centres and dark reddish-brown margins on leaf blades. Neck rot (neck blast) at the panicle base causes empty, whitish upright panicles ("whiteheads").
- **Favourable Conditions**: High relative humidity (>90%), cool night temperatures (17–20°C) with warm day temperatures (25–28°C), and high nitrogen fertilizer application.
- **Prevention & Management**: Avoid excess nitrogen applications; use split doses. Maintain good water management. Sow blast-tolerant varieties.
- **When to Contact an Expert**: If neck blast is suspected during panicle emergence, immediately seek agronomist guidance.

## Bacterial Leaf Blight (BLB) — Xanthomonas oryzae pv. oryzae
- **Crop**: Rice
- **Symptoms**: Water-soaked stripes starting from leaf tips and margins, progressing into wavy, yellowish-white lesions that cause leaf drying. In seedling stage, systemic infection leads to "kresek" (wilting).
- **Favourable Conditions**: High humidity, warm temperatures (25–34°C), strong winds, and flooding rains.
- **Prevention & Management**: Use certified disease-free seed, balanced potash application, and avoid deep flood water after storms.
