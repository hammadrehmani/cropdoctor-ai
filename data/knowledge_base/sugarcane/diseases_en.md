# Sugarcane Disease Management Guide (English)

## Red Rot — Colletotrichum falcatum
- **Crop**: Sugarcane
- **Symptoms**: Drying and withering of the top crown leaves with red discoloration along the leaf midrib. Inside split stalks, bright red tissue with characteristic transverse white patches and an alcoholic odor are observed.
- **Favourable Conditions**: Waterlogged soil, high temperatures (28–32°C), high humidity, and infected seed setts.
- **Prevention & Management**: Use certified disease-free seed sets. Practice hot water seed treatment (50°C for 2 hours) prior to sowing. Uproot and destroy infected stools immediately. Maintain adequate field drainage.

## Sugarcane Smut — Sporisorium scitamineum
- **Crop**: Sugarcane
- **Symptoms**: Emergence of a curved, black whip-like structure (flagellum) from the central growing shoot, releasing powdery black teliospores. Infected stools exhibit stunted, grassy tillering.
- **Favourable Conditions**: Dry soil conditions at planting followed by warm weather (25–30°C).
- **Prevention & Management**: Plant tolerant varieties. Rogue and burn smutted whips inside plastic bags before spores burst. Treat seed setts with hot water.
