# Sugarcane Disease Knowledge Base

## Red Rot

Caused by Colletotrichum falcatum. The most serious sugarcane disease in Pakistan.

### Symptoms
- Wilting and drying of top leaves with red discolouration of midrib
- Internal stalk tissue shows red discolouration with white patches
- Sour alcoholic smell from cut stalks
- Stalks become hollow and light

### Favourable Conditions
- Waterlogged soils
- Temperatures 28–32°C with high humidity
- Mechanical wounds from harvesting equipment

### Management
1. Use resistant varieties (CP-77-400 has some tolerance)
2. Hot water seed treatment: 50°C for 2 hours before planting
3. Avoid waterlogging — improve field drainage
4. Remove and burn infected stools
5. Do not use infected seed cane

## Sugarcane Smut

Caused by Sporisorium scitamineum. Identified by characteristic whip formation.

### Symptoms
- Black "whip" or "flagellum" emerges from growing point of infected stalks
- Whip is covered in powdery black spore mass
- Infected plants remain stunted with thin stalks
- Profuse tillering of infected stools

### Favourable Conditions
- Dry weather at planting
- Temperatures 25–30°C
- Sandy soils

### Management
1. Plant only disease-free seed cane from certified nurseries
2. Roguing: remove and destroy all whips before they rupture
3. Hot water treatment of setts: 50°C for 2 hours
4. Grow resistant varieties where available

## Ratoon Stunting Disease (RSD)

Caused by Leifsonia xyli subsp. xyli (a gram-positive bacterium). Often misdiagnosed.

### Symptoms
- No obvious external symptoms — diagnosed by laboratory testing
- Stunted ratoon crops
- Reduced internode length
- Pale green foliage
- Significant yield loss (15–30%) without visible disease

### Management
1. Hot water treatment of seed cane: 50°C for 2.5 hours (more effective than for smut)
2. Disinfect harvesting equipment between fields (bleach solution 1:3)
3. Use certified, RSD-tested planting material
