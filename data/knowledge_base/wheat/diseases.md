# Wheat Disease Knowledge Base

## Leaf Rust (Brown Rust)

Caused by Puccinia triticina. The most widespread wheat disease in Pakistan.

### Symptoms
- Small, round to oval, orange-brown pustules (uredinia) on upper leaf surfaces
- Pale yellow patches surrounding pustules
- In severe infections: entire leaf covered in pustules, leading to leaf death

### Favourable Conditions
- Temperature 15–22°C
- High relative humidity or dew
- Green crop canopy (vegetative to grain-fill stages)

### Management
1. Grow resistant varieties (Seher-2006, Faisalabad-2008, Punjab-2011)
2. Apply propiconazole 25EC (0.5 mL/L) or tebuconazole at first sign
3. Spray at booting stage preventively in high-risk areas
4. Avoid late sowing

## Yellow Rust (Stripe Rust)

Caused by Puccinia striiformis. Favoured by cooler temperatures than leaf rust.

### Symptoms
- Yellow-orange pustules arranged in stripes (parallel to leaf veins)
- More pronounced during cool, wet weather
- Can devastate entire fields rapidly in epidemic years

### Favourable Conditions
- Cool temperatures (7–15°C)
- High humidity
- Dense crop stands

### Management
1. Use resistant varieties — check NARC/PARC variety recommendations annually
2. Apply trifloxystrobin + tebuconazole (Nativo 75WG) at 0.4 g/L
3. Monitor from tillering onwards in December–February
4. Destroy volunteer wheat plants between seasons

## Stem Rust (Black Rust)

Caused by Puccinia graminis f. sp. tritici. Less common but can be devastating.

### Symptoms
- Large, dark reddish-brown pustules on stems and leaf sheaths
- Pustules rupture to release brick-red spore masses
- Weakened stems prone to lodging

### Management
1. Grow Ug99-resistant varieties where available
2. Same fungicide schedule as leaf rust (propiconazole, tebuconazole)
